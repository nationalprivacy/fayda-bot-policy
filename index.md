**Effective date:** 12 November 2026

> **In short:** We use your information only to provide the ID-printing service you request, verify payments, and maintain your bot wallet. We do not sell identity information, use it for advertising, or allow users to change official identity details. Limited account and financial records are retained as described below.

## 1. Who we are

FAYDA Print Bot ("the bot", "we", "us") is an independent Telegram-based service for customers in Ethiopia. It helps users request printable documents based on identity information retrieved through the official Fayda (Ethiopian National ID) system, following the required verification process.

The bot is **not operated by Telegram** and is **not an official service or representative of the National ID Program of Ethiopia**. It does not register identities, issue new national identity numbers, or issue government credentials. It formats verified information into the documents requested by the user.

## 2. Information we process

### Telegram account and service preferences

To operate your account, we process your Telegram user ID, your chosen language, relevant service settings, and the status of your orders. These operational details may remain while you use the bot.

### Wallet and payments

We maintain the records needed to manage your prepaid wallet, including your balance, deposits, charges, refunds, payment transaction references, and relevant transaction details returned during verification. If you submit a payment receipt or screenshot, we use it to verify the payment, not for advertising or unrelated purposes.

### Fayda verification and document generation

For an ID-printing request, the service temporarily processes:

- Your Fayda number (FAN), which identifies the record being requested.
- The one-time password (OTP) used for the Fayda verification step.
- The verified identity details returned by the official Fayda source, which may include your name, photograph, date of birth, and other fields needed for your requested document.
- The temporary document files created for delivery to you.

We use these details **only to authenticate the request, retrieve the official record, prepare the selected printable format, and deliver the result**. We do not keep the FAN, OTP, retrieved identity record, or generated document files as permanent copies after the processing workflow is complete.

For consistent document serial numbering, the service retains a one-way technical reference derived from the FAN. This reference is not a copy of the FAN or the identity record, but it is still an account-related technical identifier.

### Messages and unfinished requests

We process messages and button selections sent to the bot to respond to your requests. Limited unfinished-conversation state may be retained for approximately one day so that you can resume an incomplete step. We do not access your contacts, location, or conversations outside this bot.

## 3. How we use information

We process information solely to:

- Verify that a Fayda record can be accessed through the official verification flow.
- Generate and deliver the printable documents you request.
- Process deposits, wallet charges, refunds, and payment verification.
- Prevent duplicate payment claims and maintain accurate financial records.
- Provide order updates, respond to support requests, and keep the service functioning.
- Meet applicable legal obligations.

**We do not sell your information, use your Fayda identity details for advertising or profiling, or reuse them for unrelated services.** We do not create or modify official identity information.

## 4. Official identity verification and document integrity

The bot uses information returned by the **official Fayda source**, rather than allowing users to type or replace the identity details that appear on a document.

Access to an identity record is subject to the **OTP verification process used by Fayda**. This is intended to confirm that the request is authorized through that verification channel before the official record is retrieved.

Users cannot manually change verified names, identity numbers, photographs, dates of birth, or other identity fields through the bot. Available choices relate to presentation and printing, not alteration of the underlying official data.

The bot is a **document-formatting and printing service**, not a government identity-issuance service. Documents should not be represented as newly issued or independently certified government credentials.

## 5. Services that process information

Only the services needed to complete your request are involved:

- **Telegram:** Transmits messages, files, and interactions between you and the bot. Telegram controls its own handling and retention of content on its platform.
- **Official Fayda verification and data services:** Receive the information needed to verify the request and return the authorized identity record.
- **Document-processing service:** Temporarily processes the verified identity record to generate the selected files.
- **Payment service and payment-verification components:** Process transaction references and receipt information needed to verify deposits and handle wallet transactions.
- **Competent authorities:** May receive information if disclosure is legally required.

We do not disclose identity information to advertisers or data brokers. Any processing provider must be used only for the service purpose stated in this policy.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

**Identity data is temporary.** Once document generation and delivery processing have finished, our service does not retain permanent copies of the FAN, OTP, retrieved identity fields, or generated document files. If an order fails, temporary processing data is cleared when its retry or failure-handling workflow finishes.

**Account and financial records are different.** To maintain wallet balances, prevent payment reuse, record refunds, support transactions, and meet applicable accounting or legal requirements, we may retain your Telegram account identifier, transaction references, wallet ledger, order status, and other necessary financial records. These are not stored copies of your Fayda identity record. The derived FAN reference retained for consistent document numbering is limited to that technical purpose.

Unfinished-conversation state generally expires after approximately one day. The duration for retaining financial records depends on applicable legal and operational requirements.

Files and messages already delivered through Telegram may remain in your Telegram chat or on Telegram's systems under **Telegram's own retention and deletion controls**. Removing processing copies from our servers does not delete your Telegram chat history or copies you downloaded.

## 7. Security

We limit access to service systems and records to authorized personnel and use safeguards designed to reduce unauthorized access or disclosure. Sensitive verification and identity data is processed only for the time needed to complete the requested workflow.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. We aim to minimize what we retain and avoid keeping unnecessary copies of sensitive identity documents.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You may contact support to:

- Ask what account or transaction data we retain about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate account or payment information. Official identity fields must be corrected through the appropriate Fayda process, not through this bot.
- Request deletion of eligible account information, subject to applicable legal and financial recordkeeping obligations.
- Stop using the bot at any time by blocking it in Telegram.

Since identity information is not kept after completed processing, we generally do not have a stored copy of your completed ID document to retrieve or edit later. You are responsible for keeping files you receive through Telegram.

If you close an account with an unused wallet balance, contact support about the available balance-settlement or refund process before account closure. Account deletion does not automatically erase lawful financial obligations or records.

These choices and rights are subject to applicable Ethiopian law.

## 9. Children and changes to this policy

The bot is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Do not use it to request another person's identity information unless you have the necessary legal authorization.

We may update this Privacy Policy when our service or processing practices change. The effective date at the top shows when the published version took effect. We will communicate material changes through the bot where appropriate.

## 10. Contact us

For questions about privacy, your information, or deletion requests, use **Help → Contact support** inside the bot or contact [@NIDBOT_support](https://t.me/NIDBOT_support) on Telegram.
